Safety & privacy

Clear rules for a safer hello.

Omeet uses limited connection and moderation data to protect people using random video chat.

IP address and VPN checks

Before you can join a call, Omeet receives your public IP address through Cloudflare and checks it for known VPN, proxy, and Tor addresses using ProxyCheck. If the connection is identified as a VPN, proxy, or Tor address, Omeet will ask you to turn it off before joining. If the check service is temporarily unavailable, Omeet allows the connection to continue instead of blocking an unverified visitor. The check relies on a changing address database, so it can miss some services or misclassify a shared network.

Omeet stores IP addresses with connection timestamps, country and network provider information, session counts, moderation status, and the gender visitors select at setup. Gender is self-reported, visible only to authenticated admins, and is not shared with the person they meet. Recent visitor records without warnings or bans are removed after 90 days without activity. Warning records remain while needed to enforce the three-warning rule; gender attached to those records may remain for the same period. Permanent IP bans remain until an admin removes them.

Reports and moderation

You can report the person you are matched with. Reports may include the selected reason, your optional short note, and the IP addresses of the reporter and reported connection. Reports and admin activity are retained for up to 90 days. Admins can review connection details, issue warnings, and permanently ban an IP address after three warnings. An IP ban can also affect other people who share the same public network address.

Your calls stay peer-to-peer

Video, audio, and chat move directly between the two browsers. Omeet's admin panel does not show, record, or transcribe calls or private messages. It shows live connection metadata and the reports users choose to submit.

Questions or review requests

Contact the site owner through GitHub: bestboyssd. VPN and proxy databases can make mistakes; include the date, time, and country of the affected connection, but do not post an IP address publicly.
